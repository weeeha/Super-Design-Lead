> Customize per role / level. Each interview round tests specific capabilities. Each capability gets a rubric score with evidence.

## Round-by-round rubric

### Recruiter screen
**Tests:** logistics, compensation alignment, interest, basic communication
**Signal:** can move forward / cannot
**Time:** 30 min

### Hiring manager screen
**Tests:** career narrative, motivation, level fit, communication
**Time:** 45 min
**Score these capabilities:**

| Capability | 5 (exceptional) | 3 (meets level) | 1 (below level) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Career narrative | Coherent arc, owns choices, learns from setbacks | Tells story clearly, some self-reflection | Discontinuous story, blames external factors |
| Motivation fit | Clear reasons for our specific opportunity | Generic but believable interest | Couldn't articulate why us |
| Communication | Concise, listens well, asks sharp questions | Adequate clarity, basic questions | Rambles or dodges questions |
| Level fit (initial read) | Operating at level confidently | Approaching level | Below level for the role |

### Portfolio review
**Tests:** craft depth, decision-making, judgment, breadth
**Who:** Hiring manager + 1 senior designer
**Time:** 60 min (3-4 projects deep)

| Capability | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Craft depth | Polished + intentional, no weak detail | Mostly polished, some weak spots | Visible polish gaps, inconsistent |
| Decision-making | Articulates tradeoffs, considered alternatives | Explains decisions when asked | Can't explain why X over Y |
| Judgment | Right level of detail for context, knows when to stop | Mostly right, occasional over/under | Either over-designs everything or skips edge cases |
| Storytelling | Compels the listener, lands the why | Clear but flat | Loses the listener |
| Range | Demonstrates breadth across problem types | Two clear strengths, narrow elsewhere | One trick |

### Design exercise (take-home or onsite)
**Tests:** active design ability, process, response to constraints
**Who:** 1-2 designers
**Time:** 60-90 min onsite OR 5-7 days take-home

| Capability | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | Reframed the prompt, surfaced assumptions | Followed prompt clearly | Solved a different problem |
| Process visibility | Showed sketches, alternatives, tradeoffs | Showed final + some rationale | Only showed final |
| Response to feedback | Updated thinking, incorporated input | Considered feedback | Defensive or ignored |
| Output quality | Tight final, scoped appropriately | Adequate | Incomplete or scope-mismatched |

### Cross-functional (PM + Eng)
**Tests:** collaboration, navigating constraints, communication
**Time:** 30-45 min each
**Score these:**

| Capability | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listens to constraints | Genuinely curious, asks follow-ups | Hears them out | Pushes back too fast |
| Negotiates well | Finds the design that respects constraint AND user | Compromises | Caves or fights |
| Communicates clearly | Adapts to non-designer audience | Mostly clear | Designer-jargon only |

### Bar raiser (optional)
**Tests:** calibration check, halo/horn catch
**Who:** Senior IC from outside the team
**Time:** 60 min
**Signal:** would they hire? at what level? what's their independent read?

## Debrief process

1. **Each interviewer submits feedback BEFORE debrief, in writing, against the rubric.** No discussion before submission.
2. **Vote: strong-hire / hire / no-hire / strong-no-hire**, captured in writing.
3. **In debrief: start with dissenting voices.** Let them explain their signal first.
4. **Re-calibrate against rubric, not vibes.**
5. **Decision and rationale captured in writing.**

## Leveling decision

Before extending offer:

```
## Leveling decision — <candidate> — proposed level <X>

### Why this level (not one above)
- Specific evidence: ...

### Why this level (not one below)
- Specific evidence: ...

### Risks at this level
- Where they'll struggle: ...
- What support gets them past it: ...

### Comp range at this level
- ...

### Comparable hires at this level
- <hire 1>: similar profile
- <hire 2>: stronger in X but weaker in Y
```

If you can't articulate both why-not-one-above AND why-not-one-below with specific evidence, you don't have a leveling decision yet.

## Bias checks

Every interviewer asks before submitting:
- Was any of my evaluation about fit, vibe, background, school, or company name that wasn't job-relevant?
- Did I evaluate this candidate by the same standard as the last one at this level?
- Would I describe their work the same way if they had a different demographic profile?

Honesty here is more important than confidence.
