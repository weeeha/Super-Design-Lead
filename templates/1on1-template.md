# 1:1 Template — <name>

A starting structure for every 1:1. Adapt to length and what they bring.

## Default opener
> "What's on your mind?"

(Not your agenda. Their time first.)

## Beats (for a 30-min 1:1)

### 1. Their topics (10-15 min)
- What they brought
- Active threads they're working through
- Anything they need from me

### 2. Our threads (5-10 min)
- Open items from prior 1:1s
- Anything I need to follow up on

### 3. Career / growth (0-5 min weekly; 10-15 if due)
- Career touch (if not recently covered)
- Stretch / development thread

### 4. Feedback if held (5 min)
- Anything I owe them

### 5. Close (5 min)
- Confirm action items
- Schedule anything we didn't get to

## Default openers to break silence

If they say "nothing really, what about you?":
- "How's <project> feeling?"
- "Anything you're stuck on?"
- "Anything coming up that you're worried about?"
- "How are you doing — not the work, you?"

## Closing checklist
- [ ] Action items captured (mine and theirs)
- [ ] Next touch / follow-up clear
- [ ] Anything to escalate or schedule separately

## After meeting
Run `logging-1on1` immediately.
