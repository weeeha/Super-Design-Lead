# Coaching Conversation Template

A structure for conversations where you're working with a stuck report. See the `coaching-a-stuck-report` skill for the full methodology.

## Pre-conversation

### What I'm observing (specific)
- <specific behavior or signal>
- <specific behavior or signal>
- <when this started, roughly>

### Hypotheses I'm holding (don't share these yet)
- [ ] Skill gap — capability missing for current scope
- [ ] Motivation gap — capability present, energy absent
- [ ] Confidence gap — capability present, doubt blocking action
- [ ] Context gap — missing information / political clarity
- [ ] Mismatch — wrong role / team / level

I'll listen for cues before naming.

### Opener I'll use
> "I want to step back from project stuff for a minute. I've been noticing <specific observation>. I'm not sure what's going on for you, but I want to understand. Walk me through how things have been feeling?"

### If they say "nothing's wrong"
> "I hear you. Let me share what I've noticed and you can tell me whether that lands. <specific observation>. How does that match your experience?"

### If they want me to solve it immediately
> "Before we get to solutions, I want to understand the shape of it better. Can you tell me more about <specific thing they said>?"

### If they get emotional
> Stop the agenda. Acknowledge. "That sounds really hard. Tell me more." Don't fix. Don't pile on. If needed, reschedule the rest.

## During conversation

### Listening for (don't diagnose out loud yet)
- "I don't know how to..." → skill or confidence
- "I just don't care about this anymore" → motivation
- "I'm not sure I belong here" → confidence or mismatch
- "Nobody told me / I didn't know" → context
- "I should be doing more but..." → motivation or confidence

### Reflect to confirm
> "Let me try to play back what I'm hearing — tell me if I'm getting it right. It sounds like <paraphrase>. Did I get that?"

Repeat until they say yes.

### When ready to share my read
> "Here's what I'm hearing — I think you might be stuck in <X way> rather than <Y way>. Does that match how it feels?"

(If wrong, listen. Adjust. Don't force a diagnosis.)

## Per-flavor moves

### Skill gap
- Name the specific skill
- Pair with mentor
- Reduce scope until catches up
- Explicit checkpoints

### Motivation gap
- Schedule a career conversation (don't solve here)
- "What would make this energizing again?"
- Be open: the answer might be a role change

### Confidence gap
- Reflect actual capability back with specifics
- Set up small visible wins
- Sponsor publicly
- Suggest peer feedback to recalibrate self-view

### Context gap
- Make implicit explicit: org, who-owns-what, why-decisions-were-made
- Schedule recurring 15-min context syncs for a quarter
- Connect to peers/stakeholders

### Mismatch
- Honest: "I'm not sure this role is right for you long-term"
- Explore: role shaping, internal move, transition out (with support)
- Don't drag this out

## Close

- The flavor diagnosed (or "still uncertain — let's continue")
- Next concrete step
- When we'll check in
- What support they're getting

## After

Update report file:
```
## Coaching context
- Diagnosed: <flavor> on YYYY-MM-DD
- Conversation summary: <link to 1on1-log>
- Move: <what we're doing>
- Checkpoint: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: <progress / stalled / resolved>
```

Revisit at every 1:1 prep until resolved or re-diagnosed.
