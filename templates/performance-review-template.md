## Pre-delivery checklist
- [ ] Nothing in this review is a surprise (all themes raised in 1:1s over the cycle)
- [ ] Evidence is specific, not vague
- [ ] Gaps have a path to close, not just naming
- [ ] Calibrated with peer managers
- [ ] Delivery scheduled in a private, unhurried meeting
- [ ] Written version shared after verbal delivery, not before
